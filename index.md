# Apple: Road to Mobile
*Writing a book about Apple is almost as foolish as reading one.*

This comprehensive documentation explores Apple's evolutionary journey from personal computers to mobile revolution, tracing the key decisions, innovations, and visionary leadership that transformed not just Apple, but the entire technology industry.
